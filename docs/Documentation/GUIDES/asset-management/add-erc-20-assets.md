# Overview

The Institutional Vault accepts all ERC-20 assets, allowing you to store, view, and transact with them. The functionality may be enabled in the Settings menu.

> 📘 Note:
>
> * Only an Administrator can add the ERC-20 assets
> * The initiator of the operation needs to confirm this operation on their Institutional Vault Approver app. According to the wallet's administrative policies, additional approvals may be required before the operation is completed. If any approval request is rejected, the operation will fail.

# Add ERC-20 Assets

To add ERC-20 assets to your Institutional Vault, follow the steps below:

1. Click **Settings** on the main navigation menu.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/3555f5a7efe1909172e0130ff1a54ae9cfc449b2fbe17c77862352a3d4c8cdff-Group_18_-_2024-09-10T140320.704.png" />

2. Select the **Assets** tab.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/79ae8c05541a49bab554350e5a0ee8645e2bc56fe1256790721e8840dbd11865-Group_18_-_2024-09-12T134943.002.png" />

3. Click **Add Assets** on the top right side of your screen.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/e5d3cac5c317e3f8a3b016bc1053f14c0bbf7b8d61f116bfbacdb778446cc55f-Group_18_-_2024-09-12T135019.862.png" />

4. Enter the smart contract address and the blockchain for the ERC20 token you want to add.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/cced2f567520b981e3ff9775f260b9025e61d7688627b8fe678f599f6a919e17-Group_18_-_2024-09-12T135143.038.png" />

5. Click **Add Token** to confirm the operation.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/3b5b73871de423242200a23259ac3c4155f451a04ec86210c98ea54e0a7a9f17-Group_18_-_2024-09-12T135154.935.png" />

> 📘 Note:
>
> Once the operation is confirmed, approved, and completed, the new ERC20 token will be added to the list of available assets in the wallet.
