# Overview

To onboard the **Institutional Vault**, sign in using your company's identity provider through Auth0 in the demo environment. Ensure that your device supports biometric authentication, which is required to set up the Institutional Vault Approver app.

You can download the application from the iOS App Store (iPhone) and Google Play Store (Android). Before proceeding, verify that biometrics are enabled and fully functional on your device to ensure a smooth setup process.

# How to Onboard the Institutional Vault

## Prerequisites

* Company's identity provider through Auth0 or any Credentials that have been registered to Blockdaemon
* BD Approver app
  * [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/bd-approver-app/id6508152650)
  * [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.blockdaemon.reactnativeapproverapp\&hl=en-US)

## iPhone

To onboard the wallet on your iPhone, follow the steps below:

1. Sign in to the Blockdaemon Vault on your browser. It will show you the QR code to link the environment with your BD approver app.
2. Open the BD approver app on your iPhone.
3. Click the **Set up new environment** button.
4. Scan the QR of the Blockdaemon Vault from your browser.
5. Create a **PIN code** for authentication.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/eca7b8708b9c558630f2eeef9963b4878693de3efa033b226329bb80818c45db-Asset_3300x_17.png" />

6. Authenticate yourself by signing in using your wallet account from the BD approver app.
7. Once you are signed in, you can start approving your wallet operations.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/c38ac48a162fa98c26440e00b874980df24e9830e4365464ebc6c9f075f5d23e-Asset_4300x_10.png" />

2. Open the BD approver app on your Android.

4. Scan the QR of the Blockdaemon Vault from your browser.
5. Create a **PIN code** for authentication.
