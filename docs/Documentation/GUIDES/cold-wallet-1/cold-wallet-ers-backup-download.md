## Overview

The Cold Wallet ERS Backup Download feature offers users a secure method for creating and maintaining backup copies of their Emergency Recovery System. This process ensures that users can safeguard against the total loss of cryptocurrency stored in cold wallets.

> 📘 Note:
>
> * To use the Cold Wallet ERS Backup Download feature, ensure your cold wallet is paired with a hot wallet. To pair the wallets, please follow the steps [here](https://vault.docs.blockdaemon.com/docs/back-up-master-key).
> * The key Backup process has to be done right after the master key of the cold side is generated. 
> * If you use two nodes for the cold wallet configuration, both nodes must participate in this key backup process.

## How to Backup Master Key in Cold Wallet

To backup the master key in a cold wallet, follow the steps below:

1. Navigate to your cold wallet that has been paired with a hot wallet.
2. Select the **Settings** menu on the main navigation bar.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/20c853c-b237200f-dcf0-43ff-9872-a896d205d56a.png" />

3. Click the **Backup Key** button on the top-right of your screen.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/377fff5-66b67273-9798-4a44-85b3-197499f1137b.png" />

4. Your browser will download the backup key in **.JSON** format.
