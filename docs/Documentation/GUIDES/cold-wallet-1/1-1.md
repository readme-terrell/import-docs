## Overview

Institutional Cold Wallet offers a staking feature, allowing users to validate and confirm transactions on blockchain networks directly through the Cold Wallet(offline).

> 📘 Note:
>
> * This feature currently support **ETH** token.
> * ETH covers the transaction fees for all token transactions in the Institutional Vault. Before performing a staking transaction, ensure you have at least **32 ETH**.

## How to Stake in Cold Wallet

To stake an asset in your Institutional Cold Wallet, follow the steps below:

> 📘 Note:
>
> **Stake feature is account level only** , which means you can only use the staking feature with the specific account where it's enabled. But within that account, you're free to use different wallets to stake your assets.

1. Select the **Cold Wallet Account** you want to stake.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/de2e7c3-78114542-87eb-4347-bf3a-3e49b02635ca.png" />

2. Select the **Asset** you want to stake and click the **overflow** button.
3. Click the **Stake Asset** button.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/79d8877-38a060c1-fc9d-4f1f-a2bf-0624bddbbdb0.png" />

4. Specify all the required fields.

> 📘 Note:
>
> Ensure that you have selected the Cold Wallet account in the Source Account field.

| Field         | Description                                                                                                                            |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Asset         | It specifies the digital asset the user wishes to stake, supported by the Institutional Vault.                                         |
| From Wallet   | Indicates the wallet from which the user wants to stake assets, chosen from multiple options within their Institutional Vault.         |
| Withdraw To   | Allows specifying the destination wallet or address for receiving staking rewards or withdrawals related to the staked assets.         |
| Fee Recipient | Defines the address or wallet that will receive transaction fees. For ETH, this field automatically matches the `Withdraw To` address. |
| Crypto Amount | Used to enter the amount of the asset to be staked, indicating the number of tokens or units involved.                                 |
| Fee Rate      | Specifies the rate or percentage of staking rewards charged as a fee by the Institutional Vault or blockchain network.                 |
| Referenced    | Optional field for including references or notes related to the staking transaction, useful for record-keeping or additional info.     |

5. Click the **Stake** button.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/e87a5e0-image.webp" />

6. Confirm the operation on your **Institutional Vault Approver** application.
7. Click the **Transactions** tab, and select the **Cold Signing** tab.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/7b7b078-image_1.webp" />

8. Click the **Export Batch** button.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/7018e35-image_2.webp" />

9. Navigate to the **Cold Wallet.**
10. Click the **Transactions** tab > **Import** button.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/a2127dc-image_3.webp" />

11. Upload the transaction batch **JSON** file downloaded from the Hot Wallet app.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/61a2631-image_4.webp" />

12. After that, click the **Sign** button and click **Sign** to confirm the signing operation.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/4b0c8db-image_5.webp" />

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/3a7555e-image_6.webp" />

13. Click the **Export** button.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/8fe17f5-image_7.webp" />

14. Navigate back to your **Hot Wallet**.
15. Under the **Cold Signing** tab, click the **Accept Batch**.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/3d68210-image_8.webp" />

16. Upload the file you downloaded from the Cold Wallet app and click **Accept Batch**.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/10a8188-image_9.webp" />

17. Click the **History** to see the transaction details. The transaction status should be changed to **Broadcasting** > **Confirming**.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/8bd259e-image_10.webp" />
